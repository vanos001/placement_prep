Audit trail for the external-URL passes. Internal links, anchors, SUMMARY,
Mermaid, MathJax, fences and all DOIs are clean and checked by
scripts/validate-all.sh; THIS file tracks third-party URLs only.

MEASUREMENT NOTE. Earlier versions of this file were produced by a URL
extractor that stopped at the first ')' and bled across ']', so every URL
containing a parenthesis was measured truncated and reported dead. That
inflated the defect count. The current figures come from a Markdown-aware
extractor that keeps balanced parentheses and stops at link syntax, and from
a sweep of all 8,638 external URLs in src/.
