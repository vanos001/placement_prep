# Campus Placement Process

## Typical Process Flow

### 1. Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)
- Company presents about themselves
- **Listen carefully**: They may drop hints about interview focus
- Prepare questions to ask (shows interest)

### 2. Resume Screening
- GPA cutoff (usually 6.0-7.0+)
- Relevant skills and projects
- ATS-friendly formatting
- No typos or grammatical errors

### 3. Online Assessment (OA)
- Duration: 60-120 minutes
- Sections: Coding (2-4 problems), MCQs (CS fundamentals, aptitude)
- Platform: HackerRank, CodeSignal, HackerEarth, AMCAT
- **Strategy**: Start with what you know, manage time

### 4. Technical Interview (1-3 rounds)
- **Round 1**: DSA problems (easy-medium), CS fundamentals
- **Round 2**: Medium-hard DSA, system design basics, project deep dive
- **Round 3** (if any): Advanced topics, cultural fit

### 5. Managerial Interview (optional)
- Project experience, leadership, conflict resolution
- "Tell me about a time when..." (behavioral)
- Technical depth in your area of expertise

### 6. HR Interview
- "Tell me about yourself"
- "Why this company?"
- "What are your strengths/weaknesses?"
- Salary expectations, location preferences

## Tips for Each Stage

### Resume
- 1 page, clean formatting
- Quantify impact ("reduced latency by 40%")
- List projects with tech stack
- Tailor for each company

### OA
- Practice on the same platform (HackerRank, etc.)
- Time yourself during practice
- Read all problems first, start with easiest
- Handle edge cases

### Technical Interview
- Think out loud
- Start with brute force, then optimize
- Ask clarifying questions
- Test your solution with examples
- Discuss time/space complexity

### HR Interview
- Research the company thoroughly
- Prepare 3-5 STAR stories
- Have questions ready for the interviewer
- Be honest about what you don't know
