# Internship Preparation

Internships are the primary pipeline for full-time offers. Most large companies convert 60-80% of interns, making the internship itself the most important interview.

## The Internship Application Pipeline

| Stage | Timeline | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Research | 6-9 months before | Identify target companies and roles |
| Resume prep | 5-7 months before | Tailor resume, build projects |
| Applications | 4-6 months before | Apply early; rolling recruitment |
| OA/Coding test | 3-5 months before | Practice on platforms |
| Interviews | 2-4 months before | Behavioral + technical rounds |
| Offer decision | 1-2 months before | Compare and negotiate |

## Finding Internship Opportunities

- **Campus placements:** Register with your training and placement cell early.
- **Company career pages:** Set up alerts for internship openings.
- **Referrals:** Reach out to alumni working at target companies.
- **Job boards:** LinkedIn, Internshala, AngelList (for startups), Glassdoor.
- **Open source:** Contributing to projects can lead to internship referrals.

## Internship Interview Format

Most internship interviews are simpler than full-time loops:

| Company Type | Rounds | Focus |
|--------------|--------|-------|
| Big Tech | 2-3 coding + 1 behavioral | Algorithms, data structures |
| Service companies | 1-2 coding + 1 HR | Fundamentals, communication |
| Startups | 1-2 mixed rounds | Practical skills, culture fit |
| Research labs | 1-2 technical + discussion | Domain knowledge, projects |

## Maximizing Your Internship

### First Week
- Understand the team's goals and your project scope.
- Set up your development environment and build process.
- Schedule 1:1s with your manager and mentor.

### During the Internship
- **Deliver early wins:** Ship a small feature or fix in the first 2-3 weeks.
- **Communicate proactively:** Send weekly updates; flag blockers immediately.
- **Ask for feedback:** Don't wait until the end to learn how you're doing.
- **Document your work:** Maintain a running list of contributions and impact.

### Converting to Full-Time
- Express interest in returning early and often.
- Seek a return offer discussion 2-3 weeks before the internship ends.
- Maintain relationships with your manager and team after the internship.

## Common Mistakes

- Waiting until the final semester to start applying.
- Applying to only one or two companies.
- Not preparing for behavioral questions.
- Being passive during the internship — visibility matters.
- Ignoring company culture fit during the interview.

## Practice Questions

**Q1:** Why do you want to intern at our company specifically?
> Research the company's products, engineering blog, and recent news. Reference specific projects or technologies.

**Q2:** Tell me about a project you built outside of coursework.
> Pick a project with clear problem, technical depth, and measurable outcome.

**Q3:** How would you approach a task you've never done before?
> Break it down, research, prototype, iterate, and ask for help when stuck.
