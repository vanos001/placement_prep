# Resume Preparation

Your resume is your first impression. In tech recruiting, it typically gets **6-10 seconds** of initial scanning time. This section covers everything you need to craft a compelling, ATS-friendly resume that gets you interviews.

## Why This Matters

- Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human ever sees them
- A well-structured resume can increase your callback rate by 3-5x
- Resume writing is a skill — it improves with practice and feedback

## Quick Start

If you're building your resume from scratch:

1. Start with **Structure** to get the layout right
2. Move to **Writing Bullets** for your experience and projects
3. Review **Technical Skills** to properly list your abilities
4. Check **Common Mistakes** to avoid pitfalls
5. Run through **ATS Optimization** to ensure machine readability
6. Get feedback from peers, mentors, or online communities

## General Principles

1. **One page** — Especially for new grads and early career. No exceptions.
2. **Tailor it** — Customize for each role/company when possible.
3. **Quantify everything** — Numbers stand out to both humans and ATS.
4. **Consistency** — Same formatting, tense, and style throughout.
5. **Proofread** — Then proofread again. Then have someone else proofread.
