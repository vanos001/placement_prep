# Netflix Interview Guide

## 🎯 Netflix's Interview Process

```
Typical Netflix SWE Interview:
├── Phone Screen (1): Technical discussion
├── On-site (4-5 rounds):
│   ├── Coding Round 1: Problem solving
│   ├── Coding Round 2: System design
│   ├── Technical Deep Dive: Past projects
│   ├── Culture Fit: "Freedom & Responsibility"
│   └── Hiring Manager: Team alignment
└── Culture fit is weighted heavily
```

## 📊 What Netflix Evaluates

```
Netflix Culture Deck Values:
├── Freedom & Responsibility
│   ├── Autonomy in decision-making
│   ├── Own your work end-to-end
│   └── No process for process's sake
│
├── High Performance
│   ├── "A players" only
│   ├── Self-motivated
│   └── Consistently excellent
│
├── Context, Not Control
│   ├── Provide context, let people decide

│   ├── Shared vision, independent execution

└── Radical Candor
    ├── Honest, direct feedback
    ├── No politics
    └── Assume positive intent
```

## 💻 Coding at Netflix

### What Makes Netflix Different
- **Practical focus:** Real-world problems
- **System design weighted heavily:** Architecture matters more than algorithms
- **Depth expected:** Deep knowledge of past projects
- **Independent thinking:** Show you can make decisions

### Common Netflix Coding Topics
1. **System Design** — Most important
2. **Data Structures** — Medium difficulty
3. **Algorithms** — Practical applications
4. **Distributed Systems** — Netflix-scale

## 🤝 Culture Fit at Netflix

### What Netflix Looks For
```
Strong Culture Fit:
✅ Self-motivated, doesn't need direction
✅ Makes decisions independently
✅ Gives and receives feedback well
✅ High standards for self and others
✅ Comfortable with ambiguity

Weak Culture Fit:
❌ Needs constant direction
❌ Avoids conflict
❌ Settles for "good enough"
❌ Plays politics
❌ Resists change
```

### Common Culture Questions
1. "Tell me about a time you made a difficult decision independently"
2. "How do you handle receiving harsh feedback?"
3. "Describe a time you raised the bar for your team"
4. "What does 'Freedom & Responsibility' mean to you?"

## 💡 Tips for Netflix

1. **Show independence** — You can work autonomously
2. **Be direct** — Radical candor is valued
3. **Demonstrate high standards** — Don't settle for mediocre
4. **Know Netflix's tech** — Microservices, chaos engineering, EVCache
5. **Prepare for deep dives** — Be ready to discuss past projects in detail
6. **Ask tough questions** — Show you're evaluating them too
