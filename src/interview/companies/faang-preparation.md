# FAANG/Big Tech Interview Preparation

FAANG (Meta, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google) interviews follow a structured, high-bar process. Preparation requires 8-16 weeks of focused effort.

## Typical Process Timeline

| Stage | Timeline | What Happens |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Referral/Application | Week 0 | Apply or get referred |
| Recruiter Screen | Week 1-2 | Behavioral + logistics |
| Online Assessment | Week 2-4 | 1-2 coding problems (Meta, Amazon) |
| Technical Rounds | Week 4-8 | 4-6 rounds (coding, design, behavioral) |
| Hiring Committee | Week 8-10 | Packet review, calibration |
| Team Match | Week 10-12 | Finding the right team (Google) |
| Offer | Week 10-14 | Compensation discussion |

## How Hiring Decisions Work

### Bar Raiser

Amazon and other companies use a "bar raiser" — an interviewer from outside the hiring team who ensures the candidate meets the company-wide standard. Their vote carries significant weight.

### Calibration

After all interviews, a hiring committee reviews the complete feedback packet. They calibrate against other candidates at the same level. A single strong interview rarely compensates for a weak one.

### The Feedback Packet

- Each interviewer submits written feedback with a hire/no-hire recommendation.
- Feedback includes: problem-solving, coding quality, communication, and culture fit.
- The committee looks for consistency across rounds, not just average scores.

## Compensation Structure

| Component | Description | Negotiable? |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Base Salary | Fixed annual pay | Somewhat |
| Stock (RSU) | Vesting over 4 years | Yes — most room |
| Signing Bonus | One-time cash | Yes |
| Relocation | Moving expenses | Limited |
| Annual Bonus | % of base (varies by company) | Somewhat |

### Negotiation Tips

- **Always negotiate.** Initial offers are rarely final.
- **Get competing offers** — even a verbal offer from another company strengthens your position.
- **Focus on total compensation,** not just base salary. Stock and signing bonus often represent 40-60% of TC.
- **Ask for the level first,** then negotiate within that level's band.
- **Be professional and data-driven.** Use market data (Levels.fyi, Blind) to justify your ask.

## Preparation Plan

| Week | Focus | Daily Time |
|------|-------|------------|
| 1-2 | Data structures review + easy LeetCode | 2 hours |
| 3-4 | Medium LeetCode (arrays, strings, trees) | 2-3 hours |
| 5-6 | Graphs, DP, system design intro | 2-3 hours |
| 7-8 | Company-specific mock interviews | 2-3 hours |
| 9+ | Behavioral prep + weak areas | 1-2 hours |

## Interview Tips

- Each FAANG company has a distinct culture: Google values "Googleyness," Amazon uses Leadership Principles, Meta focuses on execution speed.
- Practice on a whiteboard or shared editor — typing speed matters less than clarity.
- For system design, pick 2-3 topics and go deep rather than preparing shallowly across many.
- After each practice session, review what you missed and add it to a spaced-repetition deck.
