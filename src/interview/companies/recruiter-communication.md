# Recruiter Communication

How you communicate with recruiters affects your candidacy at every stage — from initial outreach to offer negotiation. Professional, timely, and clear communication signals professionalism.

## Stages of Recruiter Communication

| Stage | Your Goal | Key Principle |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Initial outreach | Express interest, share resume | Respond within 24 hours |
| Scheduling | Confirm availability quickly | Give 3-4 time slot options |
| Pre-interview | Ask about format and expectations | Show preparation |
| Post-interview | Send a brief thank-you | Be genuine, not generic |
| Offer stage | Negotiate professionally | Data-driven, collaborative |
| Declining | Preserve the relationship | Be honest and respectful |

## Responding to Initial Outreach

**Template — Interested:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out. I'm very interested in the [Role] position at [Company].

I have [X] years of experience in [relevant area], and I'm particularly excited
about [specific project/product at the company].

Please find my resume attached. I'm available for a call this week — would
[Day] at [Time] [Timezone] work?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Template — Not interested right now:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for thinking of me. I'm currently focused on [reason], so I'm not
looking to make a move right now.

I'd love to stay connected — could we reconnect in [timeframe]?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Scheduling Interviews

- Respond with availability within 24 hours.
- Provide time slots in the recruiter's timezone.
- Confirm the scheduled time 24 hours before.
- If you need to reschedule, do so as early as possible with alternative slots.

## Offer Negotiation

When negotiating, communicate collaboratively, not adversarially.

```
"Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about the opportunity.
Based on my research and competing offers, I was hoping we could
discuss the compensation — specifically the [stock/base] component.
Is there flexibility here?"
```

**Principles:**
- Express enthusiasm before negotiating.
- Cite specific data (competing offers, market data).
- Negotiate one component at a time.
- Never lie about competing offers.

## Declining an Offer

Decline graciously to keep the door open.

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the offer and for the time everyone spent interviewing me.
After careful consideration, I've decided to accept another opportunity
that aligns more closely with my current goals.

I genuinely enjoyed learning about [Company] and hope we cross paths again.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Interview Tips

- Always reply within 24 hours, even if just to acknowledge receipt.
- Keep emails concise — recruiters read hundreds per day.
- If a recruiter goes silent, one polite follow-up after 5-7 business days is appropriate.
- Keep a spreadsheet tracking companies, contacts, dates, and outcomes.
